# Gold Price Forecast Tool

**6-Month Forecast: Jun-Nov 2025 using GRU Multivariate Model**
*Based on 12 months of historical data (Jun 2024 - May 2025)*

Our forecasting tool uses a pre-trained GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit) multivariate model to predict gold prices up to 6 months into the future. It analyzes 12 months of historical data to uncover sequential trends in our comprehensive feature set.
